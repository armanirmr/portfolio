# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring teams, recruiters, potential clients, and collaborators evaluating Armani Romero's software engineering work.

## Product Purpose

An interactive portfolio that helps visitors understand Armani's engineering background, inspect selected projects, and start a conversation. Success means visitors can quickly judge the work and reach Armani about a role or project.

## Positioning

The portfolio presents projects through an interactive Three.js diorama, with project details inside the laptop scene and dedicated sections for the engineer, toolkit, and contact information.

## Operating Context

Visitors use the site in a web browser on desktop or mobile. The site is a personal portfolio for a bilingual software engineer based in Colima, Mexico; visitors may be evaluating him for employment, client work, or collaboration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Built with Vite, React, and Three.js.
- Visitors can navigate by selecting the 3D objects or using the section navigation.
- The Work section presents Changeweft, Baja Coordinator Services, and Fulikuli / FLKL. FLKL links to https://flkl.vercel.app/.
- The Engineer section presents Armani's profile. The Toolkit section presents his tools. Get in Touch provides a direct email action and address-copy action.
- Contact address: armanirmr@gmail.com.
- The site has a motion toggle and respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Do not list Salesloft as one of Armani's projects.
- Current 3D assets are user-provided GLB files in `public/models/`, including laptop, Morrillo character idle/wave/look/point animations, and toolbox idle/open/close animations.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly chose Persona 4 Golden as an aesthetic reference and specified The Golden color palette. Treat these as references for the portfolio, not as an official affiliation with the game.

## Evidence on Hand

- Project names and details currently represented in `src/main.jsx`: Changeweft, Baja Coordinator Services, and Fulikuli / FLKL.
- User-provided 3D GLB assets are present in `public/models/`.
- No testimonials or measured project outcomes are currently established; do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Make real project work easy to inspect.
- Keep navigation playful while keeping project and profile information readable.
- Represent Armani's projects and experience accurately.
- Make it straightforward to contact him.
